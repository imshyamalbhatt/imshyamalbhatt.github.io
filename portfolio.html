Content & On-Page SEO

  • Own on-page optimization and page-hygiene maintenance across an assigned portfolio of country and city-specific virtual phone number pages and competitor comparison pages
  • Refresh older landing pages and blog posts that still show traffic potential, and retire ones that no longer do
  • Draft and publish new blog posts and landing pages from keyword-researched content outlines, spanning international dialing guides, phone number format guides, and product and feature explainer content
  • Write competitor comparison articles positioning CallHippo against rival VoIP and call center platforms
  • Produce informational and buyer's guide content, including cost guides and vertical-specific use case pieces
  • Add original material, credentialed author bios supporting E-E-A-T, and dedicated case study sections to existing content
  • Write thought leadership and problem statement style articles
  • Add FAQ, HowTo, and Article schema markup to improve rich result search visibility
  • Run AI-generation and plagiarism checks on drafts before publishing
  • Build and maintain internal links between blog posts and pages to preserve the site's topical silo structure
  • Post newly published articles to LinkedIn, and author original LinkedIn thought leadership pieces
  • Rewrite YouTube video titles and descriptions for SEO
  • Support content and internal-linking updates tied to seasonal campaigns such as Black Friday

Off-Page Link Building & Digital PR

  • Run a repeatable backlink outreach process: identify top-ranking competitor pages, analyze backlink profiles and anchor text patterns, compile target site contacts, and conduct outreach for link placements
  • Benchmark competitors' backlink profiles against each other to prioritize outreach targets
  • Secure backlink placements on third-party sites using anchor text themed around core product terms
  • Craft outreach messaging and follow-up sequences for link exchange and guest placement campaigns
  • Source and vet backlink opportunities using Google Search Console and Ahrefs, screening candidate domains against authority and spam-score criteria
  • Run a seasonal Black Friday link-building and digital PR push, targeting relevant SaaS companies for placements in external roundup content
  • Pursue additional placements through competitor comparison roundups and other referring blogs
  • Run an ongoing structured link exchange program
  • Track brand mentions earned through outreach and campaign work
  • Audit the backlink profile and flag low-quality links for disavow
  • Maintain a log of acquired and incoming backlinks

Community & Social

  • Engage on Quora around genuine user problems rather than direct brand promotion, prioritizing high-ranking questions on target keywords
  • Grow CallHippo's follower presence on Quora
  • Build and grow an authentic Reddit community presence (r/CallHippo) through consistent content and engagement
  • Identify and answer relevant Reddit threads on targeted keywords using social-listening tooling (Octolens)
  • Create a regular cadence of Reddit content mixing product-feature, review-style, and thought-leadership posts
  • Work to grow the subreddit's visitor base, post views, and followers
  • Maintain a general brand presence on Twitter/X

Technical SEO

  • Correct faulty redirects and broken or dead links across the site
  • Resolve site silo-structure issues through large-scale internal-linking audits
  • Resolve Google Search Console indexing errors across affected pages
  • Coordinate fixes for Core Web Vitals (Largest Contentful Paint) issues across the page portfolio
  • Monitor and resolve crawl errors flagged by Ahrefs site-audit tooling
